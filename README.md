# Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing the health risks associated with smoking.  
It explores the impact of smoking on major organs — **heart, lungs, liver, and kidneys** — using patient demographics, smoking habits, and health indicators.  
The dashboards compare **healthy vs. damaged organ states**, providing clear, data-driven storytelling through visualizations.

## 📈 Key Features
- **Organ-specific dashboards**: Heart, Lungs, Liver, Kidneys  
- **Healthy vs. Damaged views** for comparative analysis  
- **Demographic insights**: Age, Gender, BMI distribution  
- **Smoking behavior metrics**:  
  - YOS (Years of Smoking)  
  - CPD (Cigarettes per Day)  
- **Risk analysis**: Cholesterol and Hypertension levels across age groups  
- **Interactive navigation** with anatomical visuals for better storytelling  

## 🔍 Important Insights
- Nearly **30% of patients are current smokers**, with significant variation across age groups.  
- **Men show higher smoking prevalence** compared to women across all categories.  
- **Cholesterol and hypertension risks increase with age**, especially in patients with longer smoking histories.  
- **Liver and kidney dashboards reveal stronger correlations** between smoking duration and elevated BMI/health risks.  
- **Healthy vs. Damaged organ views** emphasize the long-term impact of smoking on cardiovascular and respiratory health.  

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Demonstrated
- **Power BI**: Dashboard design, data modeling, DAX calculations  
- **ETL processes**: Data cleaning, transformation, and integration  
- **Data storytelling**: Turning complex health data into clear, actionable insights  
- **Visualization techniques**: Pie charts, line graphs, bar charts, and anatomical illustrations
